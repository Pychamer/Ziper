TO ADD YOUR IMAGES:
==================

1. Copy your images to: assets/images/
2. Open: design.html
3. Find: <div class="image-placeholder">
4. Replace with: <img src="assets/images/your-image.jpg" alt="Description">
5. See ADDING-IMAGES.md for detailed instructions

EXAMPLE:
--------
<img src="assets/images/prototype.jpg" alt="Prototype">
<img src="assets/images/animation.gif" alt="Animation">
<img src="assets/images/concept.png" alt="Concept">
