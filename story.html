Our Story

How we chose this project

Why We Created This

This invention touches both of our hearts. It beat the many inventions that we had thought of. This can greatly help people who have issues with remembering or staying consistent with taking their medication.

The Beginning

This began when we tried thinking of a global invention that would not only help a certain age group. This medication tool can be bought by anyone who needs to take the littlest prescription.

Versatile Design

Many sizes of medication boxes fit inside this container - sprays, pills, and liquids mostly fit. You don't have to put your pills one by one, just put the whole box.
