Our Story

This invention touches both of our hearts. It beat the many inventions that we had thought of. This can greatly help people who have issues with remembering or staying consistent with taking their medication.

This began when we tried thinking of a global invention that would not only help a certain age group. This medication tool can be bought by anyone who needs to take the littlest prescription. Many sizes of medication boxes fit inside this container - sprays, pills, and liquids mostly fit. You don't have to put your pills one by one, just put the whole box.

Development Stages

1. Concept & Sketches

Initial brainstorming and hand-drawn sketches to visualize the medication reminder system.

2. 3D Modeling

Creating detailed 3D models in Blender to refine dimensions and functionality.

3. Prototyping

Building and testing prototypes to ensure the container fits various medication sizes.

4. Testing & Refinement

Continuous improvement based on feedback and real-world testing scenarios.
